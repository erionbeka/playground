# Consent and Pilot Protocol Approval Procedure

This procedure defines the approvals required before Playground Life is used with real pilot participants or real clinic/child/caregiver data.

## Approval Gates

Pilot launch requires documented approval for:

- Pilot protocol.
- Consent/assent materials, where applicable.
- Data categories and purpose.
- Data retention, deletion, and export policy.
- Security/data protection review.
- Backup/restore and disaster recovery procedure.
- Incident response policy.
- Access review and least-privilege procedure.
- Publication/de-identification plan.

## Consent Content Requirements

Consent materials should explain:

- What Playground Life does.
- What data is collected.
- Why data is collected.
- Who can access the data.
- How long data is retained.
- How deletion/export requests are handled.
- Whether data may be used for feasibility/usability reporting or publication.
- Known risks and safeguards.
- Contact information for support, privacy questions, and withdrawal.
